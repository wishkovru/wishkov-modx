Wishkov Button
==============

Adds the [[WishkovButton]] snippet: a "В вишлист" (add to wishlist) link for the current page.
A visitor clicks it and the page is saved to their wishlist on wishkov.ru. The button is a plain link:
no scripts and no requests from your site to ours until the click.

Usage: [[WishkovButton]] or [[!WishkovButton? &theme=`outline`]]
Defaults are in System Settings, namespace "wishkovbutton".

About Wishkov
-------------
Wishkov (wishkov.ru) is a free wishlist service from Russia: a person writes wishes down, shares one link,
and friends reserve gifts between themselves so that nobody buys the same thing twice. The extra is made by
the team that runs the service. The service itself is in Russian; the default button label is "В вишлист"
and can be changed with the &label property or the wishkovbutton.label setting.

Why Wishkov
-----------
- Free for everyone: no paid plans, cards or SMS.
- Guests reserve gifts straight from the link without signing up.
- A product is added by link: name, price and photo are filled in automatically, from marketplaces and regular shops.
- A wishlist is ready in half a minute on a phone or a computer.
- Surprise mode hides reservations from the owner; an anti-wishlist lists what not to give.
- 21 themes and own photos, a one-tap story for social networks, friends, birthday reminders, shared wishlists.
- Wishes need not be products, and a "Gift ideas" catalog helps when you are out of ideas.
- Nothing runs on your MODX site: the snippet outputs a link, no scripts and no trackers.
